A [metauni](https://metauni.org) seminar I ran in 2023 on statistical mechanics, with a view 
towards understanding apparent phase transitions in deep learning. The speakers were 
Rohan Hitchcock and [Ben Gerraty](https://bengerraty.github.io).

## Talks

- "What Is Statistical Mechanics? Hamiltonian Systems and Liouville's Theorem." Rohan Hitchcock. *3 August 2023.* [notes](/stat-mech/intro.pdf) · [video](https://youtu.be/D2Lyxkf5gIM)
- "Entropy and the Boltzmann Distribution." Rohan Hitchcock. *17 August 2023.* [notes](/stat-mech/entropy-and-boltzmann.pdf) · [video](https://youtu.be/DwyIcfzT8e8)
- "Stochastic Processes." Rohan Hitchcock. *31 August 2023.* [notes](/stat-mech/stochastic-processes.pdf) · [video](https://youtu.be/sa4y1G4VHyQ)
- "Stochastic Differential Equations." Rohan Hitchcock. *14 September 2023.* [notes](/stat-mech/stochastic-integration-and-sdes.pdf)
- On Seung, Sompolinsky and Tishby (1992), "Statistical Mechanics of Learning from Examples." Rohan Hitchcock. *28 September 2023.* [notes](/stat-mech/statistical-mechanics-of-learning-from-examples-notes.pdf)
- "Statistical Field Theory 1: 1D Ising Model to Spin-1/2, Part 1." Ben Gerraty. *30 November 2023.* [notes](https://bengerraty.github.io/notes/SFT1-2.pdf)
- "Statistical Field Theory 1: Introduction and 1D Ising Model to Spin-1/2, Part 2." Ben Gerraty. *14 December 2023.* [notes](https://bengerraty.github.io/notes/SFT1-3.pdf)
- "Statistical Field Theory 2: 2D Classical Ising Model to 1D Quantum Ising Model, Part 1." Ben Gerraty. *18 December 2023.* [notes](https://bengerraty.github.io/notes/SFT2-1.pdf)
